02 · cookies

cookies & tracking

Last updated: 24 April 2026 · Version 2.0

the short version

this site sets no tracking cookies. we use Plausible for analytics, which doesn't use cookies or collect personal data. you don't need to click a banner. there isn't one.

what cookies are

small text files some websites store on your device. they can be useful (remembering a login) or invasive (following you across sites). we don't use the invasive kind.

what this site sets

CookiePurposeDuration
None from us——

that table is not a joke. unbarrier.me itself sets no cookies.

what other services might set, on their own pages

  • Stripe checkout — when you click "book" and are taken to Stripe's page, Stripe sets its own cookies for payment security. these are on stripe.com, not unbarrier.me.
  • Google booking — when you pick a session time via Google Appointment Schedule, you're on Google's domain. their cookies, their policy.
  • YouTube / Vimeo — if we ever embed a video, those may set cookies. currently we don't embed any.

analytics — Plausible

we use Plausible to count page visits. it's cookie-free, privacy-respecting, and doesn't identify you. it counts things like "247 people read the vision page this week" — never "Jane Doe visited at 14:03". full details in their data policy.

managing cookies in general

you can block, clear, or manage cookies in your browser settings. here's aboutcookies.org for step-by-step guides. since we don't set any, this won't affect how unbarrier.me works — but it's a good habit.
