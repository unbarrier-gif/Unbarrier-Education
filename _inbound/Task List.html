01 · Booking stack

Booking — link-out with a styled bridge

If not embed, what?

Link-out, wrapped in a branded interstitial. When someone clicks "Book" on a session, they land on /book/[session-id] on your site — a small, styled page in your brand ("You're heading to Google Calendar to pick a time · takes 30 seconds · click to continue"). Then the CTA link-outs to Google Appointment Schedule. Keeps brand moment, keeps accessibility, stays fast. Cost: £0. Claude Code builds the interstitial in Phase 2.

Paid alternatives if you ever want full embed-without-the-cost:
  • Cal.com — £12/mo, EU-hosted, accessible, theme-able to your brand, embeds cleanly. Best upgrade path later.
  • SavvyCal — $12/mo, nicer UX, less accessibility focus.
  • TidyCal — $39 lifetime, cheap but rougher.
Ship on Google Appointment Schedule + styled bridge. Migrate to Cal.com if/when you outgrow it.
