TidyCal Setup Pack.

Single source of truth for booking, payments, and confirmation copy. TidyCal is the front door — it auto-creates Stripe products and processes payments. You build it once from this doc and don't touch Stripe directly.

How TidyCal + Stripe actually fit together

TidyCal connects to your Stripe account once. After that, every paid booking type you create in TidyCal automatically creates the matching Stripe product and payment intent. You don't build anything in Stripe directly, you just monitor revenue there. Subscriptions (Accessible Coaching) live in TidyCal's Subscriptions tab and run on Stripe Billing under the hood. This is why we deleted the separate Stripe Product Catalogue — it was duplicating work TidyCal does for you.

Payment — three tiers

How TidyCal handles 3 prices on one event

TidyCal doesn't have native price-tiers on one booking page. Cleanest pattern:

  • Three duplicate booking types, all pointing at the same date/time/Zoom link. This works because TidyCal counts all three against the same calendar slot.
  • Slug them: guest-stage-[topic]-access (£15) · guest-stage-[topic] (£25, the canonical link to share) · guest-stage-[topic]-supporter (£40)
  • On the public sessions page, the £25 link is the primary CTA. The page description tells visitors the other two tiers exist and how to choose.

Page 04 · Accessible Coaching — per-client subscription + booking page

Accessible Coaching · per-client subscription + 1:1 page · from £55/mo

Sustained 1:1 support — two sessions a week + WhatsApp between. Per-client: each client gets their own TidyCal subscription, their own unlisted booking page, their own WhatsApp thread. Unlimited "instances" of this template, but never more than 5 active clients.

Internal positioning — for your eyes, never client-facing

For ND people who are spiralling or need help to land, then hold their space. The work is in making coaching itself accessible to ND life — sessions move, weeks slip, the WhatsApp thread holds the gap. Application-by-conversation. 3–5 active clients ever. £55/mo is the Gemma template (Phase 1 floor); price is set per client at intake based on shape of need (more sessions, heavier WhatsApp, longer sequence — adjust up).

How it works in TidyCal — two pieces, working together

PieceWhereWhat it does
1. The Subscription
(payment side)		 TidyCal → Subscriptions One subscription product per client. £55/mo (or per-client agreed amount). Sends them a single Stripe-backed payment link. They sign up once → billed monthly → cancellable any time. This is just billing, not booking.
2. The Booking Page
(scheduling side)		 TidyCal → Booking types A 1:1 booking page marked unlisted / hidden. Set to FREE (no payment at booking — they're already paying via subscription). They bookmark this page and self-book their two slots a week from your live calendar.

The two pieces never directly link to each other inside TidyCal — they're connected by the human handshake. After a discovery conversation, you send both links in one email. They subscribe via Link 1, then bookmark Link 2 and start using it.
