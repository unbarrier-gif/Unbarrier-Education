Version 3 · Longer (if he wants fuller picture)

For a proper briefing

Unbarrier (unbarrier.me) is my coaching and learning business for neurodivergent adults. After years running my own creative consultancy as a multiply neurodivergent person myself (dyslexia diagnosed at 10, ADHD & dyscalculia at 20), I saw how much the generic coaching world fails people who think differently. Unbarrier is my answer to that.

What I sell:

  • Loop Breakers — small-group coaching sessions (6–8 people, 90 mins, £45/seat). Includes guest-hosted sessions with other practitioners, with a revenue share.
  • 1:1 coaching — £120/session, 60 minutes. Online via Google Meet.
  • Free discovery calls — 30-min intros for people deciding whether to work with me.

How the site works: newsletter sign-ups go through MailerLite; booking and calendar handled by Google Appointment Schedule; payments through Stripe. Analytics are Plausible (privacy-friendly, no cookies). Hosted on Vercel.

What I'm not: a therapist, a medical practitioner, or a diagnostic service. That's made explicit in the Terms. People come to me for coaching — thinking, strategy, accountability — not clinical support.

Audience: majority UK, some international. Ltd company registered England & Wales. Launching in the next few weeks.
