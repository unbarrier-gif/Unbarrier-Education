Who Loop Breakers is for —
and why now matters more than everyone.

A reference doc to keep every Loop Breakers asset (sessions, copy, guest-host invites, blog posts, social) tonally and strategically anchored. If a piece of work doesn't speak to the woman described below, it doesn't ship. Anything outside the room belongs to broader Unbarrier work — kept warm on the roadmap, not built first.

01 · The Loop Breaker

The woman this room was built for.

She isA neurodivergent woman running her own small business.

She's somewhere in her thirties or forties. Diagnosed early, late, self-diagnosed, undiagnosed-but-suspecting — ADHD, autistic, AuDHD, dyslexic, dyscalculic, the whole spectrum. All stages welcome. No diagnosis required. She's a coach, a consultant, a designer, a maker, a teacher who's left the classroom, a freelancer, a founder of one. She earns a living by herself, for herself, on her own terms — and that part is non-negotiable.

She has been circling the same idea for months. A landing page she keeps rewriting. An offer she can't ship. A pricing change she can't bring herself to send. A pivot that's been three browser tabs deep since January. She's not lazy and she's not stupid — she's running on a brain that wasn't built for the productivity systems she keeps trying.

She's tired of rooms that ask her to mask. She's tired of advice that assumes she just needs to focus. What she actually needs is 90 minutes in a room of women who get it — and one next step she'll actually take.

02 · What we promise her

One sentence. Hold every piece of copy against this.

Loop Breakers is the room where a neurodivergent woman with her own business can stop circling, be properly understood for 90 minutes, and leave with one next step she'll actually take.

If a session, an email, a blog post, or a guest pitch doesn't make that promise more credible to her — cut it. Anything else is for a different room.

03 · In the room · not in the room

Who Loop Breakers is for — and who it isn't (yet).

In the room

  • Neurodivergent women running their own micro-business (1-person, occasionally 2-3)
  • Diagnosed early, late, self-diagnosed, or undiagnosed-but-curious — all stages welcome, no diagnosis required
  • Coaches, consultants, designers, makers, freelancers, ex-teachers gone independent
  • Stuck on something specific — a launch, a page, an offer, a price, a pivot
  • Tired of productivity advice that doesn't fit their brain
  • Want a small room, not a webinar, not a course
  • Will pay £10 for a Tuesday Loop Breakers seat to be properly heard (£5 access seat on request, no questions). Wednesday Guest Stages run separately at £25 sliding (£15 / £25 / £40)

Not in the room (yet)

  • Teachers and educators — Nici's heart is here, the work is coming, but not first
  • Edtech / Apple PL / school-leadership audiences — Unbarrier serves these elsewhere
  • Corporate L&D / DEI buyers — different proposition, different price
  • People looking for a course, a programme, or 1:1 coaching (those exist separately)
  • Neurotypical productivity-curious — kind, but not who this is built for
  • People who want big rooms and case studies
  • Free-event seekers — the £10 Tuesday ticket (with the £5 access seat for those who need it) is the filter, deliberately. Wednesday £25 standard is doing the heavier lift.

"Not in the room yet" is not a rejection. It's a sequencing decision. See horizon below.

05 · The test — for any new asset

Before you publish, send, or ship anything.

The 4-question check

  1. Does it speak to the woman in §01? If a teacher would feel addressed but a stuck ADHD coach wouldn't — rewrite for the coach.
  2. Does it deliver on the promise in §02? Stop circling, be heard, one next step. Concrete, not aspirational.
  3. Does it use her language? "Stuck" not "blocked." "Circling" not "ideating." "The thing I keep avoiding" not "your KPI." If you can imagine her saying it back to a friend — it's right.
  4. Does it respect her brain? No urgency-pressure. No "limited time." No countdowns. The pricing structure (Tuesday £10 with £5 access seat · Wednesday £25 sliding £15/£25/£40) and capped seats (10 hard cap on Tuesdays, up to 40 on Wednesday Guest Stages) + Founding Member offer are the only forms of scarcity allowed, because they're real.

If a piece of work fails any of these — it isn't bad, it's for a different room. Park it. Use it for Unbarrier-broader work later. Don't ship it under the Loop Breakers banner.

06 · Where this drifts — and how to catch it

The pattern to watch for.

The drift looks like this: a piece starts off written for the Loop Breaker, but somewhere in the middle it slides into talking about teachers, schools, Apple PL, edtech, accessibility consulting — because that's where Nici's deepest expertise sits, and so the assistant (or Nici, or anyone helping) reaches for those examples by default.

Recent example: Nicki Hambleton's first guest-host letter framed her as "already has the audience shape — Apple Professional Learning educators, creative-practice-curious teachers". Both true. Neither relevant to the Loop Breakers room. The fix: re-anchor the letter in why she belongs in front of the actual audience — her visual thinking, her octopus-and-rabbit-holes self-awareness, her own struggle to start things — not her teaching credentials.

The catch: if you find yourself writing about Nici-or-her-collaborators' credentials rather than their recognition by the room — pause. The Loop Breaker doesn't care about Apple Distinguished Educator. She cares whether the woman across the screen has been stuck in the same way she's stuck.

07 · One thing this doc is not

This isn't a forever-narrowing of who Nici works with.

Unbarrier serves a wider audience: educators, schools, accessibility-buying organisations, conference stages. That work pays bills, builds reputation, and matters. It just isn't Loop Breakers.

The discipline this doc enforces is: when you're working on a Loop Breakers asset, the only audience you serve is §01. When you're working on Unbarrier-broader work, the audience widens. Two different rooms, deliberately.
