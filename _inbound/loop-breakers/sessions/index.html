You're not on your own after

The 90-day loop — what happens after the session.

You don't book a session and disappear. You get three things — gentle, useful, no spam.

Day 0

Session notes

A one-page recap of your idea, your next step, and a link to your breakout partners' notes. In your inbox within 24 hours.

Days 1–30

The WhatsApp group

A small, moderated WhatsApp group for the cohort — where you already are, no new app to learn. Ask a question, share a win, request a nudge. 30-day access.

Day 60

One nudge

A single "how's it going?" email with your original one-next-step attached. No guilt, no dashboard. Just a check-in.

Day 90

Invitation back

Either as an attendee with a new loop, or — if you're ready — as a guest host.
