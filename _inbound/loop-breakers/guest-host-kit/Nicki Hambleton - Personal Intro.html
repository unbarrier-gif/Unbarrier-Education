All stages welcome. Women who've been quietly going round in circles on the same idea for months and haven't found a room that gets it. Not teachers (yet — I want to do that too, in time). Not edtech. This room. The one I needed and couldn't find.

The reason I want you in this room isn't because of the Apple PL credentials or UWCSEA or any of that. It's because of the way you think — out loud, in pictures, octopus-arms-down-rabbit-holes — and the fact that you've struggled to start things you knew you wanted to start. The women in this room have lived that. They'll recognise you immediately.

A session I'd love to co-craft with you

Proposed working title:

"Sketch-noting for joy (and for thinking) — drawing as a way out of the loop"

90 minutes. For micro-business owners who've been circling an idea for months and can't write their way out of it. You teach drawing-as-thinking. They bring a pen and the thing that's stuck. By the end of the session, the tangle is on paper and there's one obvious next step.

This is not sketch-noting for educators. It's sketch-noting for the woman who has rewritten her landing page eleven times and still can't ship it.

I'd come to the prep call with a running order draft. You edit, keep what's yours, ditch what isn't. The admin weight is on me — you turn up and do what you already do, to an audience pre-filtered for exactly this.
