Part 1 · Your 25-minute checklist

Five steps, in this order

Step 1

Sign up at MailerLite

~3 minutes

  1. Go to mailerlite.com → Sign up free
  2. Use nici@unbarrier.me as the account email
  3. Answer the onboarding questions honestly (business type: coaching/education; list size: under 100)
  4. Skip the "import subscribers" step
  5. They'll ask for a business address — use 45–47 The Triangle, Malmesbury, Wiltshire, SN16 0AH (required by anti-spam law, shown in email footers)

Step 2

Verify sending domain

~5 minutes + DNS wait

  1. Left sidebar → Settings (cog icon) → Domains → Add domain
  2. Enter unbarrier.me
  3. MailerLite shows you SPF and DKIM DNS records
  4. Add them to your DNS host (Cloudflare / Google Domains / wherever unbarrier.me is registered)
  5. Click Verify. Usually green in 1–15 minutes.
If the verify step stalls: screenshot the DNS records MailerLite is asking for and tell me where you host DNS — I'll translate them into exact clicks for your host.

Step 3

Create the Form

~3 minutes

  1. Left sidebar → Forms → Create form → Embedded form
  2. Name it: Unbarrier newsletter
  3. Pick any template — we don't use MailerLite's styling, our site has its own
  4. In the form builder → Settings:
    • Double opt-in: OFF
    • Success message: "You're in. Check your inbox — first note arriving now."
  5. Save and note the form ID — it's in the URL: app.mailerlite.com/forms/{FORM_ID}/edit

Step 4

Build the Welcome Automation

~10 minutes

  1. Left sidebar → Automations → Create automation
  2. Name: Welcome
  3. Trigger: When subscriber joins a form → pick "Unbarrier newsletter"
  4. Add Email step → paste Email 1 (below) → set delay: immediately
  5. Add Delay: 3 days. Then Email → paste Email 2
  6. Add Delay: 7 days. Then Email → paste Email 3
  7. Top-right: switch automation ON

Step 5

Test & tell me

~2 minutes

  1. Subscribe yourself at a test email (different from your account email). Email 1 should arrive in under 60 seconds.
  2. Paste back into chat: Form ID + "domain verified ✓" + any test-email weirdness
