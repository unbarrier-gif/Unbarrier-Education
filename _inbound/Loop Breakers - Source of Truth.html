01 · The three products

Three distinct rooms. Three distinct prices. Don't blur them.

Loop Breakers is three products under one banner. They have different formats, capacities, prices, and emotional registers. They're not interchangeable, and copy that conflates them weakens all three.

Tuesday Loop Breakers — the regular room

Small, slow, held

Hosted by Nici. 90 min. 10 seats hard cap. Online (Google Meet). Tuesday mornings. The accessible weekly floor — the room you can afford to keep coming back to.

Wednesday Guest Stage — the premium room

Bigger, themed, expert-led

Hosted by a guest practitioner. 90 min. Up to 40 seats. Online (Zoom or Meet). Off-cadence Wednesdays. The expert is the value — talk + held Q&A, not small-group coaching.

Accessible Coaching — the held one-to-one

Sustained, weekly, low-friction

1:1 with Nici. 2 sessions per week + WhatsApp voice/text in between. From £55/month, set per client at intake. 3–5 clients max, ever. 6-month sequence then review. Sessions can be moved month to month — if it's consistent we look at why, no shame. Per-client TidyCal page at /[firstname]-coaching, unlisted. Still part of the Loop Breakers community. Gemma is the first client (£55/mo template).

Do not blur

Don't say "Loop Breakers, sometimes with guest hosts." Say "Loop Breakers (Tuesdays, with Nici), Guest Stage (Wednesdays, with invited practitioners), and Accessible Coaching (1:1 sustained, with Nici)." Three products. Three pages. Three booking flows. Accessible Coaching is not the same as a one-off 1:1 — see §02.

Locked until Phase 3 review

Tuesday £10 does not move until Loop Breakers reaches 20+ regulars on a weekly cadence (Phase 3 trigger). Even then, the £5 access seat stays. Wednesday £25 sliding scale is reviewed at Phase 2 trigger (10+ regulars on Tuesday).

Why £10 / £25 / £55 — and not £15 / £20 / £45

Because the products do different jobs. Tuesday is the weekly room — what makes it work is people keep coming, and £10 is the price that lets that happen for someone with ADHD-tax inconsistent income. Wednesday is the event room — paying for a named expert's time and prep — and £25 is honest about the value of that. Accessible Coaching is the held one-to-one — £55/month for two weekly sessions plus WhatsApp between them is below market for sustained 1:1 work, deliberately, because the people who need it most are often the ones who can least afford it. Capping at 3–5 clients is what makes that price possible without it eating Nici alive.

03 · Capacity caps

10 hard. 40 soft. No exceptions on the 10.

Tuesday — hard cap

10 seats

Including Nici. Booking closes at 10. Waitlist after that. Do not bend this — the small-room feel is the product. Eleven people is a different room.

Wednesday — soft cap

Up to 40 seats

"Up to 40." Could be 6, could be 35. Format works at any size — guest gives talk, takes held questions. Don't promise the host a full room. Don't promise the audience a small one.

The 10 isn't arbitrary

Eight is the comfortable working number. Ten is the "we sold out, plus one or two, still feels held" number. Twelve is too many — you stop being able to remember who's saying what, and the implicit invitation to speak gets diluted. 10 is the wall.

05 · Audience phrasing

All stages welcome. No diagnosis required.

Loop Breakers is for neurodivergent women running their own small businesses. The audience is broad in diagnostic stage and tight in life stage:

Use this phrasing — verbatim or close

Diagnosed early, late, self-diagnosed, undiagnosed-but-curious — ADHD, autistic, AuDHD, dyslexic, dyscalculic, the whole spectrum. All stages welcome. No diagnosis required.

Use this for "who's in the room"

Founders, educators, writers, parents, designers, therapists, consultants — people with ideas that have been waiting. All neurodivergent (ADHD, autistic, dyslexic, AuDHD or somewhere in that terrain). All stages welcome. All warm, smart, and slightly tired of productivity advice.

Do not say

  • "Late-diagnosed adults" as the headline audience description — too narrow, excludes early-diagnosed people like Nici herself.
  • "Women only" as a hard rule — phrasing is "for neurodivergent women" because that's who books and who the room is built for, but bookings aren't gender-policed.
  • "Coaches and consultants only" — the room includes makers, teachers gone independent, parents, anyone running something for themselves.

06 · Nici's bio & permission lines

The reframe is the brand.

Nici is not late-diagnosed. Diagnosed dyslexic at 10; ADHD, dyscalculia, and Auditory Processing Disorder at 20. This matters because it's the truth, and because it reframes the whole "neurodivergent founder" cliché — Nici has known her brain her whole adult life. What was missing was how to build around it. That's what Loop Breakers teaches.

Micro-bio — one line, for signatures, footers, flyers

I'm Nici — diagnosed dyslexic at 10, ADHD & dyscalculia at 20, still looping. These are the sessions I wish I'd had.

Short bio — for session pages, about footer

I'm Nici. I've spent years circling ideas I couldn't quite land — until I built the kind of room I wished I'd had. Loop Breakers is that room. I run most sessions; a few rotate to other neurodivergent practitioners who bring their own frameworks.

Reframe — for about page, brand story, media

I'm not late-diagnosed — I've known I think differently since I was ten. What I didn't know, for years, was how to build around it. Dyslexia at 10. ADHD, dyscalculia, and Auditory Processing Disorder at 20. So I went into adulthood with a label and no system — and spent two decades looping on the same handful of good ideas. Loop Breakers is the room I needed. The one that doesn't ask you to be a different kind of brain.

The reframe is the brand position

"Diagnosed since ten. Still looping." This is the line that separates Loop Breakers from every other "neurodivergent founder" coaching offer. Don't soften it. Don't generalise it back to "late-diagnosed." Use it.

10 · Review triggers

When to revisit this doc.

  • Phase 2 trigger — when Tuesday Loop Breakers reaches 10 weekly regulars. Revisit: §02 (Wednesday £25 → likely £35), §07 (guest fee Phase 1 → Phase 2).
  • Phase 3 trigger — when Tuesday reaches 20+ regulars on weekly cadence. Revisit: §02 (Tuesday £10 → first time it moves), §07 (Phase 2 → Phase 3 base+rev-share).
  • New guest host signed — revisit §07. Add their name, fee, first session date. Make sure their personal letter exists in loop-breakers/guest-host-kit/.
  • Domain change, company name change, ICO registration confirmed — revisit §08.
  • Bookings pattern shifts (e.g. Wednesday Guest Stages start drawing 50+ requests) — revisit §03 capacity.
  • Diagnostic story changes (e.g. you decide to pursue an autism assessment) — revisit §06.
