03b · Booking form questions — what to ask, why, when

The intake form is the first sentence of the relationshipWhat do I need? Accommodations? — answered.

You asked what a bookee might want to know before they say yes, and what you need to know to host them well. This is both: the questions you ask them on the booking form, and the questions they'll ask you that the form copy + confirmation email need to pre-empt.

The principle, in one line

Ask what you'll actually act on. Don't ask out of politeness. Every question on the form has to either (a) change how you set up the session, or (b) change how you welcome that person. If neither — cut it. unbarrier-style accessibility means using the answers, not collecting them.

Form 1 · Loop Breakers group session (Vision→Launch + guest sessions)

Used on both live booking pages right now. Keep it short — 6 questions max. People bouncing off a 12-field form is the #1 cause of TidyCal abandonment.

The booking page copy — pre-empting questions they're scared to ask

These aren't form questions — these are the FAQs that should sit on the page itself, above the form, so people don't have to email you to find out. Each addresses a real reservation a ND adult might have before clicking book:

For the group session page

Six things people want to know before they book

  • "Will I have to talk?" — No. Cameras off, mic muted, lurk-and-learn is welcome. We name this in the room.
  • "Will I have to draw / write / share my screen?" — Only if you want to. Most prompts are private. We share if it's helpful, never to perform.
  • "What if I have to leave halfway?" — Fine. Door is unlocked both ways. Recording goes out after.
  • "What if I'm late?" — Come in whenever. We don't shame the door.
  • "Refund policy?" — Full refund up to 24h before. After that, you get the recording + credit toward the next session.
  • "What if I can't afford £10?" — Email me, just say "£5 seat please." No questions, no form. The £5 access seat is real and routine.
For the 1:1 page

Three things £120-clients want to know

  • "What if 1 hour isn't enough?" — We'll say so by minute 50. You can book a follow-up at a discount, or we'll wrap with a written summary.
  • "Will you record it?" — Only if you ask. Default is no recording, written notes shared after.
  • "What's your specialism?" — Educator + ND adult + Apple PL trainer. If your loop is genuinely outside that (e.g. corporate finance, surgery), I'll say so before we book.

Decision 12 (locked) · Phase 1 ticket & guest-fee pricing

Tuesday Loop Breakers · £10 flat · £5 access seat on request. Held at this price for all of Phase 1 and Phase 2. The £10 floor is the access promise — it doesn't move until weekly cadence with 20+ regulars (Phase 3 trigger).

Wednesday Guest Stage · £25 standard · sliding £15 / £25 / £40. Attendee picks at checkout. Up to 40 seats (Guest Stage format). Reviewed at Phase 2 trigger.

Guest host fee: £75 per session · Phase 1 flat fee. Paid within 7 days, regardless of attendance. Rises to £125 at Phase 2 trigger, £200 + 50% of revenue above base at Phase 3.

Reasoning — why two prices, not one:

  • Tuesday and Wednesday do different jobs. Tuesday is the weekly room — what makes it work is people keep coming. £10 is the price an ADHD-tax inconsistent income can absorb every week. Wednesday is the event room — paying for a named expert's prep and time. £25 is honest about that value.
  • Bundling them at one price would break both. £15 for Tuesday is too high to be the floor. £15 for Wednesday is too low to fund a real guest fee plus margin. Two products, two prices.
  • £75 is what Phase 1 can honestly pay. Even at 3 attendees on Wednesday (£25 standard × 3 = £75), the guest fee is covered. At 10 attendees, +£175 net. At 20, +£425 net. The £75 fee never depends on a sold-out room.
  • Founding Members protect the floor. The £180 Founding rate is locked at signup. Any future price rise grandfathers them in — that's the deal.
