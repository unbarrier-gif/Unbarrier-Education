Three questions before you start

You don't need a plan for the technology yet. You need to know what you're solving.

Sit with these three before we talk. On your own, or with your team. Ten minutes.

  1. 01

    What does it look like in your classrooms on a good day?

    Start with the lesson, not the app. If you can describe the good day, you can tell whether the technology got you nearer to it.

  2. 02

    Who on your staff is still working around the tech rather than with it?

    Every school has them. They're not the problem, they're the measure. The workaround is the data.

  3. 03

    How would you know it had reached the child?

    Installed is easy to prove. Reached is the harder question, and it's the one almost nobody asks.

No right answers. Bring what you've got.
