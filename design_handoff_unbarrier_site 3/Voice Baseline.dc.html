Every readiness tool scores the organisation. unbarrier.voice measures the one thing they skip: whether the technology, the access and the communication actually reach the learner they were bought for. Device-agnostic. Built on the learner's own experience. It is the measurement layer under everything unbarrier does.

What a baseline is

Where you are now, scored against the seven questions overleaf. A starting number, not a verdict.

It is a baseline you can measure again later, so "impact" stops being a word and becomes a number you can stand behind.

In the cycle every piece of our work runs on — notice → design → try → embed — voice is what makes notice and embed into measurements rather than impressions. It is the same instrument at both ends, which is the only reason the difference between them means anything.

Where it sits

unbarrier.voice is what unbarrier.audit and unbarrier.access both run on. A discovery day uses it to find the gaps. A partnership year uses it to prove the movement between the start and the end. Same seven questions, every time.

Onboarding: the voice baseline, and the first plan we agree together. The end of the year: the endline against the baseline. After that, renewal on evidence, not goodwill. If the numbers didn't move, that is the conversation we have first.
