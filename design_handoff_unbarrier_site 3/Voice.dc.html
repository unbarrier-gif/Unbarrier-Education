v4 · what a baseline is · from the voice baseline paper, 13 sep

what a baseline is

where you are now, scored against the seven questions. a starting number, not a verdict.

it is a baseline you can measure again later, so "impact" stops being a word and becomes a number you can stand behind.

in the cycle every piece of our work runs on — notice → design → try → embed — voice is what makes notice and embed into measurements rather than impressions. it is the same instrument at both ends, which is the only reason the difference between them means anything.
