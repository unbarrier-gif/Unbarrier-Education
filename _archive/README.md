# _archive

Material belonging to other people, kept out of the served site.

Nothing in this folder is part of the website. Next.js only serves `public/`,
so nothing here is reachable over HTTP, indexed, or included in a build.

It exists because Loop Breakers was retired (28 Aug 2026) and the guest-host
pages were deleted with it. Those pages carried work by two guest hosts. That
work is theirs, so it was archived here before deletion rather than destroyed
with the routes.

| Folder | Whose | What |
|---|---|---|
| `nicki-hambleton/` | Nicki Hambleton | 5 sketch-notes, her portrait, and the invitation letter Nici wrote to her |
| `gemma/` | Gemma | her portrait, and the invitation letter Nici wrote to her (includes a voice-note quote in her own words) |

**Both letters contain private fee and commercial terms.** They were previously
served at unguessable, `noindex` URLs under `/loop-breakers/guest-host-kit/`.
Treat them as private correspondence.

Intended use: Nici sends each person their own folder. Once that is done this
folder can be deleted — the images are large (~36MB) and serve no purpose in
the repo afterwards.
