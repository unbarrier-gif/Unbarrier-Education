*A note before the kit — for Nicki*

## Nicki, a room built for what you already do.

I want to be straight with you about who this room is for, because the audience is what makes this work.

**Loop Breakers is for neurodivergent women running their own small businesses** — diagnosed early, late, self-diagnosed, undiagnosed-but-curious; ADHD, autistic, AuDHD, dyslexic, dyscalculic, the both-or-five-at-once. All stages welcome. Women who've been quietly going round in circles on the same idea for months and haven't found a room that gets it. Not teachers (yet — I want to do that too, in time). Not edtech. *This room.* The one I needed and couldn't find.

The reason I want *you* in this room isn't because of the Apple PL credentials or UWCSEA or any of that. It's because of the way you *think* — out loud, in pictures, octopus-arms-down-rabbit-holes — and the fact that you've struggled to start things you knew you wanted to start. The women in this room have lived that. They'll recognise you immediately.

## A session I'd love to co-craft with you

### Working title (yours, kept):

*"Sketch-noting for joy (and for thinking)"*

90 minutes. For micro-business owners who've been circling an idea for months and can't write their way out of it. You teach drawing-as-thinking — and you've already shown me the running order you'd want.

**Settle first** — that quiet line work you did in your workshop, a meditative few minutes before anyone has to think hard. **Then surface the tangle** — boxes, simple shapes, getting whatever they're stuck on out of their head and onto paper. **Then the path** — metaphor drawing, the mountain or the rabbit holes or whatever lands. They leave with the tangle visible, in shapes they can move and label, and one obvious next step.

This is *not* sketch-noting for educators. It's sketch-noting for the woman who has rewritten her landing page eleven times and still can't ship it. *You teach for 60. I hold the Q&A for the last 30.*

I'd come to the prep call with a running order draft. You edit, keep what's yours, ditch what isn't. The admin weight is on me — you turn up and do what you already do, to an audience pre-filtered for exactly this.

## Why I think you specifically — for *this* room

- **You think the way they think.** Visually, in maps, in multiple starting points. The women in this room are tired of being told to write a list. They need someone showing them another way.
- **Your pace matches ours.** Loop Breakers isn't a hustle format. Neither are you.
- **You've been the woman with eleven rabbit holes too.** That's not a credential, it's credibility — and it's what this room is hungry for.
- **A Loop Breakers session is also a clean asset for you** — a recording, a framework tested with a non-teaching audience, evidence your work travels beyond classrooms. (For the teacher version later, this is good practice ground.)

## The date I'm holding for you

### Wednesday 20 May 2026 · 10:30–12:00 BST · 90 min · Zoom

I know we floated 19 May and that didn't work — so I've moved to the day after. *Wed 20 May* is the version I'm going to write the rest of the launch around if you say yes.

It's a one-off Wednesday. Loop Breakers normally runs on Tuesdays — that's the cadence I'm building. But I'd rather flex one week to have *you* in the launch month than wait until June. Future guest sessions will land on Tuesdays; this is the only Wednesday in the schedule.

This is a *Guest Stage* session — bigger than my regular Loop Breakers rooms. Regular rooms are capped at 10 (hard), to keep them properly small. *Guest Stages can take up to 40* — same care, less round-the-room, more talk + held Q&A. Honestly I don't expect to fill 40 first time out. The point of opening it wider is to *see what we get* when the audience is yours, not just mine.

## One thing to say plainly — this isn't a one-off in my head

I want you to know I'm holding May 20 as *the first session*, not the only one. We talked about a series, about regular slots, about the asset pack you'd design, about what your work might do inside Loop Breakers over time. None of that needs deciding now — and I don't want this letter to pin you to anything beyond May 20.

But I also don't want you reading the letter and thinking I'm only thinking one session ahead. I'm not. The bigger conversation is open whenever you want to have it — on the prep call, after May 20 lands, or in a separate chat with proper time. Your call.

## And — looking ahead — a smaller, premium version of you

### Phase 2 idea (not promised, just floated):

*"Drawing Studio with Nicki Hambleton"* — 6 seats, 3 hours, sketchbook + pen kit posted to each attendee's door, *£180 per seat*. Once a quarter. Premium product, premium fee, the version of your work that needs the small room.

The Guest Stage in May is the entry point. The Drawing Studio is the upgrade path for the people who want more of you. We'd build that one once we've seen the May session land — but I want you to see the shape now, so the road forward is clear.

## What I'd ask of you this week

Just two things. Read the kit (link below — the deal, the timeline, the boundaries, all of it). Then tell me *yes, maybe, or not for me* on Wed 20 May. One line is enough. If it's a yes or a maybe, I'll book us a 30-min prep call and we go from there.

If it's a not-for-me, or a not-this-shape-but-something-else, that's also great information. I'll take it.

## On the money — said straight (and honestly)

**£75 for the 20 May session.** Paid within 7 days, no waiting.

I want to be honest with you about this number, because you've earned that honesty. *£75 is a Phase 1 fee, not a market fee.* It reflects exactly where Loop Breakers is right now — small, just-launched, tickets at £10. It's the fee I can pay you without it costing the business something it can't afford yet, and without me quietly subsidising it from a personal account that frankly doesn't have it to give.

The thing I won't do is offer you a number I can't sustain — because you'll smell that, and so will I, and we'll both feel weird about it. Better to start at a number that works, and have it grow with the room.

What I'm committing to *in writing* is that the fee scales with the room, on triggers we both agree:

- **Phase 1 (now)** — £75 per session. Tuesday Loop Breakers at £10 (£5 access seat). Wednesday Guest Stages — like ours on 20 May — at flat **£25** (no access tier on Wednesdays).
- **Phase 2 (when we hit 10 Tuesday regulars + 2 sold-out Guest Stages in a row)** — your fee rises to **£125**. Wednesday tickets move to £35. Tuesday stays £10 — that's the access promise.
- **Phase 3 (subscription model, weekly cadence, 20+ members)** — **£200 base + 50% of revenue above base**. Renegotiated in writing before we get there.

The trigger criteria are written down. I'll show them to you on the prep call. *You're not subsidising my growth long-term — but Phase 1 is what it is, and I want you in the room while it still feels like Phase 1.*

## A small thing

If 20 May doesn't work after all — June, July, August all work too. We're aiming to ramp up slowly. There's no pressure for the first one to be yours, even though I'd love it to be.

## And the teacher sessions — for the record

I haven't forgotten. The Apple PL / educator-facing version of this is a thing I want to build with you, and it's on the roadmap. But not first. Right now my whole focus — and the only audience Loop Breakers is being built for in 2026 — is neurodivergent women in micro-business. The room I needed when I was circling. Once that's solid, the teacher rooms come next, and you're my first call. Saying it out loud so we both know it's not lost.

With real warmth,

*— Nici*
